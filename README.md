This is a little 'Papers, Please' inspired game, made using Godot 4 for a 5-day game jam hosted by my Digitech teacher with the theme 'Man vs. Machine'.
Your job is to vet people entering a secure human settlement in the aftermath of an AI takeover, flushing out the AI agents attempting to gain access by impersonating humans. 

Content warnings:
- Contains some unsettling/uncanny imagery and body horror themes.
- Contains themes of authoritarianism and fictional propaganda.
- Contains themes of loss of identity, paranoia and impersonation.

NOTE: as of writing, I have not created a web, MacOS or Windows build for this game; you will have to open it in Godot. Issues may occur when opening it in a version of Godot other than 4.6. Enjoy!
