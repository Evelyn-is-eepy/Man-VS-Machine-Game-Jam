This is a little 'Papers, Please' inspired game, made in 5 days as part of a game jam hosted by my Digitech teacher with the theme 'Man vs. Machine'.
Your job is to vet people entering a secure human settlement in the aftermath of an AI takeover, flushing out the AI agents attempting to gain access by impersonating humans.
Content warnings:
- Contains some unsettling/uncanny imagery and body horror themes.
- Contains themes of loss of identity, paranoia and impersonation.

Enjoy! (and burn down an AI data center)
